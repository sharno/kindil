Welcome to kindil

kindil means lantern: قنديل.

This is the reader. Put EPUB and plain text books in the Kindle's books folder, over USB or over SSH, and they show in the Library.

Tap the right of the page to go forward and the left to go back, or swipe. Tap the bar at the top for the front light and the text size.

kindil remembers your place in every book, the text size and the light, across restarts.

The reader shows text: PDFs, books with DRM, and EPUB styling and images are not supported.
